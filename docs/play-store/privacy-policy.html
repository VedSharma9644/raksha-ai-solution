Privacy Policy

App: Amrat Security Agency
Android package: com.vedashtech.amratsecurity
Operator: Vedash Tech
Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the Amrat Security Agency mobile app handles information. The app is for security guards and staff whose agency has issued them access. It is not a public social app and it is not directed at children.

1. Who this applies to

It applies to people who install and sign in to Amrat Security Agency on Android. Your employer or security agency decides that you may use the app and provides your Guard ID or registered mobile number.

2. Information the app uses

Depending on how your agency uses the service, the app and its server may process:

  • Account details: mobile number or Guard ID, and the password you enter to sign in. The password is checked through Firebase Authentication. The app does not keep your password in its own database.
  • Work profile: name, employee code, phone number, assigned site, post, and shift times provided by your agency.
  • Location: precise or approximate location when you punch in, punch out, or complete an interval check-in, so the app can confirm you are at the assigned site. Location is used at those times. The app does not continuously track you in the background.
  • Camera and attendance photos: a live selfie taken in the app for punch-in, interval check-in, or punch-out. These photos are uploaded with the attendance record. The attendance camera flow does not ask you to pick a photo from the gallery.
  • Attendance records: date, time, site, geofence result, and whether a shift is open or ended.
  • Leave, relief, and pay information: leave requests you submit, relief or handover requests, and payroll information your agency makes available in the app.
  • Notifications: local reminders and, where enabled, push notifications about duty, check-ins, and related work alerts. This can include a device notification token.
  • Technical data: basic device and app information needed to sign in, call the service, and keep the session working.

3. Why this information is used

  • To sign you in and show your assigned duty.
  • To record attendance and confirm you are at the correct site.
  • To let you request leave or relief and to show work information your agency stores.
  • To send duty reminders and operational alerts.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix failures.

The app is an employment and site-security tool. Your agency is the organisation that assigns your duty and reviews your attendance.

4. Permissions

Android may ask for:

  • Camera — attendance selfies.
  • Location — site check at punch-in, punch-out, and interval check-in.
  • Notifications — duty and check-in reminders.
  • Internet — to reach the Amrat Security Agency service.
  • Photos or storage on older Android versions — only if the system requires it to save or send an image. Current attendance capture uses the camera.

You can deny a permission in Android settings. If you deny camera or location, attendance actions that need that permission will not complete.

5. Where information is stored

Account, duty, and attendance data are sent to the Amrat Security Agency service operated for Vedash Tech, hosted on Google Cloud (including Cloud Run, Firebase, and Cloud Storage) in the Asia South (Mumbai) region unless your agency’s deployment says otherwise. Attendance selfie images are stored in cloud storage and are scheduled for deletion after about 120 days. Employment records such as attendance logs, leave, and profile data are kept while your agency account is active and for as long as the agency needs them for operations, payroll, and legal duties.

6. Who can see it

Your agency’s authorised admin and HR users can see the duty, attendance, leave, and relief information needed to run sites. Vedash Tech and its hosting providers process the data only to run the app. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. Sharing

Information may be shared:

  • with your employer or security agency;
  • with Google Cloud and Firebase, which host the service;
  • if a law, court, or competent authority requires it;
  • if needed to investigate misuse or to protect staff, sites, or the service.

8. Children

Amrat Security Agency is a workforce app for adult security personnel. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices

You may ask your agency administrator to correct your profile, or to close your access when you leave the job. You may turn off notifications, camera, or location in Android settings. Closing those permissions can stop attendance features from working. To ask a question about this policy, use the developer email shown on the Google Play listing for Amrat Security Agency.

10. Security

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Traffic to the service is sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after the update means you accept the revised policy.
